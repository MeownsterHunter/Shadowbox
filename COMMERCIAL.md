# Commercial licenses

Shadowbox is free under two public licenses. [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) explains them in a table.

You need a commercial license when you want to use Shadowbox commercially and you do not want to follow the AGPL. In practice that means making money with it while keeping your own source closed. For example:

- shipping Shadowbox, or anything built from it, in or with a closed product that is sold,
- preinstalling it on hardware that is sold, without meeting the AGPL's terms for the software on it,
- running it as part of a paid service without offering the service's users the source.

You do not need a commercial license if your use is noncommercial, or if you follow the AGPL in full.

To ask about a commercial license, email meownsterhunter@digital-haven.net with who you are and what you want to do. Terms are agreed case by case.

If you are not sure which side you are on, ask. Asking costs nothing.
