# License

Shadowbox is offered under three licenses. You pick the one that fits what you are doing. You only need to follow the one you pick.

## Which one is for me?

| If you are... | Use this license | What it asks of you |
|---|---|---|
| Using it for personal, hobby, study, research or other noncommercial purposes | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSES/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md) | No commercial use. When you pass it on, include the license terms (or a link to them) and the "Required Notice" line from [NOTICE](NOTICE). |
| Adding it to a project under the GPL or AGPL (version 3), or willing to follow the AGPL | [GNU Affero GPL, version 3 or later](LICENSES/AGPL-3.0-or-later.txt) | If you give anyone a work that combines Shadowbox with your own code, or let people use it over a network, they must be able to get the source of that combined work under the same kind of license. |
| Using it commercially without publishing your own source | A commercial license, see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) | Agree terms with the licensor first. |

## The short version

- **Free for noncommercial use.** Use it, change it and share it, as long as it is not commercial.
- **Free for GPL and AGPL projects.** If your project is under version 3 of the GPL or the AGPL, Shadowbox can be part of it under the AGPL.
- **Paid for closed commercial use.** If you want to make money with it and keep your own source closed, you need a commercial license.

## Common cases

- *I am tinkering at home.* Noncommercial license. Nothing to do.
- *I maintain a free emulator under the MIT license.* Noncommercial license, as long as your project is noncommercial.
- *I maintain an emulator under GPL version 3, or version 2 "or later".* AGPL. The GPL version 3 allows combining with AGPL code, so you can bundle Shadowbox. Shadowbox's part stays under the AGPL.
- *My project is under GPL version 2 only.* Neither public license fits. Get in touch.
- *I want to ship it in a device or program I sell, with my own source closed.* Commercial license.
- *I want to ship it in something I sell, or run it as a service, and I will give my customers or users the source of the combined work and meet the AGPL's other terms.* The AGPL allows that.
